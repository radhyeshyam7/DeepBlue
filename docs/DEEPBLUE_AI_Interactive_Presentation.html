Chapter 1: The Vulnerability Landscape

Why Existing Perimeter Defenses Fail Against UPI Scams

⚡ Instant Push Settlement Vulnerability
  • Immediate Finality: Funds move between central bank accounts in under 1,000ms with zero post-debit chargeback window.
  • Perimeter Blind Spot: Because the victim types their legitimate UPI PIN, device binding, SMS OTP, and biometric sensors treat the transfer as 100% authorized.
  • Mule Velocity: Funds are cascaded through layered mule accounts and liquidated via ATMs within 5 to 10 minutes.
🎭 The 4 Primary Scam Archetypes
  • Electricity Disconnection Urgency: Threatening immediate blackout at 9:30 PM unless a bill is transferred to a scammer's VPA.
  • Authority Impersonation: Posing as police officers, tax agents, or bank fraud managers to coerce verification debits.
  • Cognitive Inversion: Sending collect requests or reverse QR codes, claiming entering a PIN is needed to 'receive' money.
  • Cognitive Duress: Victims exhibit anomalous hesitation, multiple amount edits, and nocturnal transaction timing.

Chapter 3: Feature Engineering

Dynamic Baselines & Interaction Biometrics

📊 User-Relative Baselines

Amount Deviation: Ratio $A_i / \mu_u$ comparing transaction amount against the user's 30-day spending mean.

Statistical Z-Score: Measuring standard deviations $(A_i - \mu_u) / \sigma_u$ to adapt dynamically to high-wealth vs student accounts.

⏱️ Temporal & Velocity Profiling

Hourly Burst Velocity: Rolling 60-minute window tracked in Redis to intercept rapid retry attacks.

Diurnal Hour & Day: Flagging high-risk nocturnal transactions (1:00 AM - 4:00 AM) typical of social engineering scams.

🎯 Client Interaction Biometrics

Hesitation Score ($\tau_{\text{hes}}$): Temporal delay between entering the amount and confirming the payment.

Edit Count ($C_{\text{edit}}$): Number of backspaces and corrections in the input field while being guided by a scammer.

Chapter 4: Core Machine Learning Engine

Unsupervised Isolation Forest Anomaly Scoring

🌲 The Mathematical Principle

Anomalies are "few and different"—they isolate near the tree roots with short path lengths $h(\mathbf{x})$.

s(x, n) = 2^(- E[h(x)] / c(n))
  • Zero-Day Protection: Requires no historical fraud labels, detecting newly minted scam narratives immediately.
  • 100 Estimators: Robust ensemble averaging across orthogonal hyperplanes.
🚦 Calibrated Risk Tiers
Score 0 – 30: LOW (ALLOW)
Instant frictionless authorization for nominal everyday payments.
Score 31 – 70: MEDIUM (WARN)
In-app modal displaying explainable fraud warnings with user acknowledgment.
Score 71 – 100: HIGH (DELAY)
Payment withheld; cooling-off delay enforced, secondary verification required.
Chapter 5: Transparency & Explainability

Dual-Layer XAI & Natural Language Reason Engine

🔍 TreeSHAP & LIME Surrogates
  • Exact Shapley Contributions: TreeSHAP allocates game-theoretic additive importances $\phi_j(\mathbf{x})$ across feature dimensions.
  • LIME Verification: Fits local linear surrogate boundaries around transaction perturbations to guarantee decision robustness.
  • Inference Optimization: Pre-compiled background distribution enables sub-10ms SHAP value extraction.

Chapter 6: Enterprise Platform Engineering

100% Free & Open-Source MLOps Lifecycle

📦 DVC & Pandera

Data versioning via `dvc.yaml` guarantees reproducible pipelines (`dvc repro`). Pandera schemas block malformed or out-of-range payloads.

🔄 Apache Airflow

Automated scheduled DAG orchestration (`fraud_ml_pipeline.py`) executing ingestion, preprocessing, scaling, training, and evaluation.

🧪 MLflow Registry

SQLite-backed experiment tracking logging model parameters, run IDs, performance metrics, and versioned serialized model joblibs.

📊 Prometheus & Grafana

Live telemetry scraping `/metrics` to provision a 6-panel real-time visual dashboard tracking latency, score distributions, and drift.
